# Changelog

What changed between SynVoy versions. Results of different versions are not comparable in detail, so keep the version and the commit that `./run_synvoy.sh` prints with your results (for example `SynVoy v1.0.1` and `main@1281e47`).

## Unreleased (changes after 1.0.1, November 2026)

Counted calls can differ from 1.0.1. These points change which genes the search finds or how it labels them: the rescue beside a single neighbour, the length floors, refused hit chains in significant blocks, hull models judged in their place, the masked-round model floor and the places E_syn counts, the runs without a home GFF, and easy mode's choice of related genomes. The strong-hit answer adds records that are never counted.

- **A strong hit without synteny gets an answer, on by default and never counted.** A GOI hit outside every searched GOI zone (block ± the GOI margin) is tried when the terms of a high-confidence call hold in the whole genome: E ≤ 1e-6 over the genome, at least 0.7 of the query covered (with `core_completeness`, which the `signal_anchor` module switches on, 0.7 of the query or of its core, the protein without signal peptide and GPI tail, as the HIGH label reads it), no other home protein within 10 bits. Its model must be judged HIGH there, must not be a retrocopy (no model exon across an exon junction of the home gene, unless the exon is long enough to hold an intron that miniprot read as coding) and must not be frame-broken without an intron. The retrocopy test needs the home gene's exon junctions: those of the annotated home gene or, where the home step built its own miniprot model (no home GFF, or no annotated gene that matches the query), those of that model when it has canonical splice sites (GT or GC, then AG) at every junction; and it needs a miniprot model of the candidate made with the home protein. A candidate it cannot test is no answer (`retrocopy_untested`), unless the home gene model has one exon, where there is nothing to test. It is written as MEDIUM, `GOIClass=genome_wide_strong_hit`, never seeds the next wave and is not in the gene tree. The report keeps at most one per genome, only where no call is counted: the headline names it as "strong hit(s) without synteny (not counted)", or as "strong hit(s) near a block, outside its GOI zone (not counted)" when it lies in a block's searched window, and the genome is listed under `strong_hit_without_synteny` or `strong_hit_near_block`. It takes no part in choosing the regions or the stretch a figure shows: the figures draw it outlined where it lies inside that stretch, and the anchor grid lists it otherwise. The web result page lists it apart, not among the weak matches. With `--decoy_mode` its decoys are searched in the whole genome too (`decoy_rate.genome_wide`). `--goi_genome_wide_path false` switches the step off. In 1.0.1 the path was off by default and, when on, added counted calls. `--goi_genome_wide_without_blocks` is removed.
- **A strong GOI hit beside a single neighbour is searched.** The dispersed-GOI rescue is chosen against the blocks: a hit inside a block's GOI zone is left to that block, and a hit outside every zone seeds a block of its own inside the envelope of three or more neighbours, or within the GOI margin of a neighbour on a sequence with fewer. Before, a strong hit beside a neighbour that formed no block was searched by nobody. When the block cap binds, it keeps the gene-seeded blocks and those holding such a hit first. Models found only this way carry `DispersedRescue=1`. The limit count `dispersed_near_anchor` is now `dispersed_in_goi_zone` (hits inside a searched GOI zone).
- **Length floors count aligned query residues.** MMseqs2 writes nucleotides in its alignment-length column, so every length floor held an MMseqs2 hit to about a third of its value. The floors now read the aligned query span, the same for MMseqs2, tblastn and Smith-Waterman.
- **A refused hit chain is judged in a significant block.** Where miniprot finds no model, a chain of GOI hits that was too short or covered too little was refused; in a block with E_syn ≤ 0.1 it is now labelled by its E-value like every candidate (`RefusalLifted=`), and it never seeds.
- **Hull-rescue models are judged in their place** (the core or window of a significant block), not always in the whole genome, so they can carry a higher label.
- **More GOI copies; one place per gene in E_syn.** A later miniprot round keeps a model below 0.3 × the best score when it lies on a hit no other model explains. When E_syn counts the places of a flanking gene, its hits within the gene's own gap limit are one place, so the exons of one gene no longer count as several: E_syn falls and more blocks can be significant.
- **Without a home GFF**, the annotation transfer uses targets named `.fa`, `.fasta` or gzipped too, and the flank walk keeps to the stretch SynVoy annotated around the locus.
- **Easy mode** ranks related assemblies by assembly level before NCBI status and gives a dropped low-quality pick's place to the next candidate.
- **The start of a run stops** on easy mode given `--home_genome`, `--home_gff` or `--target_genomes`, on a listed target genome or annotation that matches no file, on a query shorter than `--min_query_length`, and on a `--max_flanking_distance` that is negative or no number (given on the command line, the value now works; it stopped the flank walk before).
- **The blocks of a genome are searched side by side.** New parameter `iterative_search_block_workers` (`auto`: as many blocks at once as the genome has CPUs; a number: at most that many; `1`: one after another, as in 1.0.1). The calls, records, decoys and log lines are those of one block after another; only the time and the memory change. On the Candida pilot (4 CPUs) the search step is 1.9 times faster with the same CPU time. Each block worker starts as a copy of the genome's worker and shares the genome in memory, but runs its own MMseqs2 window search (about 0.9 GiB on the pilot, whose peak rises from 1.9 to 3.5 GiB). A retried search task searches the blocks one after another. Nextflow's trace adds up the memory of every process, so its `peak_rss` counts the shared genome once per worker and is higher than what the task uses.
- **Runs.** A default run fits a laptop with 8 GB (PHYLO_SORT asks for 4 GB). Every search needs parasail and stops at its start without it. MMseqs2 failures are read on both output streams: a split limit too small for a genome records the genome as not searched (`mmseqs_split_memory_limit_too_small`), with a message naming `--mmseqs_split_memory_limit` and `--iterative_search_memory`; MMseqs2's other memory messages take the lower rungs. The searches print MMseqs2's errors at every verbosity: `--mmseqs_verbosity 0` acts as 1 and gives a warning. New parameter `mmseqs_max_seqs` (300, MMseqs2's default). The gene tree uses one thread by default (`compute_tree_cpus 1`), so a rerun gives the same tree. Under a conda profile the search step gets the packages of `environment-ml.yml` when a language-model or structure layer is on, and mamba is used only when it is on the PATH. `logs/` holds only the current run's tasks.
- **Report.** A model counts once (the rescue passes' models were counted twice). The LOW models are listed apart in `goi_low_records`, never counted. `goi_found_but_not_syntenic` leaves out only genomes with a counted call or a kept answer. The gene-family advisory counts records another home gene explains as well. In the region step a distant region is `goi_dispersed_macrosynteny` only where the search modelled the gene there as HIGH or MEDIUM, else `goi_hit_no_call`; a region holding a counted model of the search within 20 kb is always written, and gene-bearing regions of MEDIUM or better without a counted call are listed with the reason (`regions.goi_regions_without_call`). The hits the rescue did not take are listed with strand, query span, bits and the test they failed; the GOI rows of each genome's search are kept in `goi_hits/`. A model found from a seeded tandem copy also carries its identity, coverage and bits against the home protein. Removed: `total_raw_search_hits`, `genomes_with_raw_search_hits`, `synteny_results.synteny_hits_count`, the hit entries of `staging_diagnostics` and the paralog-confusion keys. Deprecated: the class-based `ambiguous_goi_annotations`, `resolved_goi_annotations` and `goi_ambiguous_only_genomes`.
- **Figures.** Each record of the report is drawn once: a model the report merged into another is no longer drawn as a copy of its own. A counted call outside the stretch a row draws is listed in the anchor grid; a record made only of rescue models is still in no figure. The legends say what they draw.
- **Removed settings** (a run that still sets one warns or stops, as `docs/PARAMETERS.md` § 20 says): `goi_genome_wide_without_blocks`, `allow_missing_smith_waterman`, `discovery_paralog_margin`, `structural_max_length`, `disable_paralog_check` and `paralog_confusion_min_gap`, the last two with the reciprocal-best paralog check, which could flag nothing. Script options removed: `resolve_effective_params.py --user_override` and `generate_report.py --paralog_confusion_min_gap`. The profiles `test`, `test_melettin` and `test_tetramorium`, which needed a folder the repository does not hold, are removed.
- **Decoys** are shuffled with a seed from the decoy kind and the protein sequence, not the gene's name: the decoy rows of a decoy-mode run differ once from 1.0.1.
- Messages, `--help` texts and config comments no longer cite working notes outside the repository; `install.sh` ends with the launcher's commands.

## 1.0.1 (11 November 2026)

Documentation only: working notes that were never meant to be public left the repository. A run computes exactly what it computes with 1.0.0.

## 1.0.0 (11 November 2026)

The first release, commit `b3e3202`.

The version file has said 1.0.0 since 13 October 2026, so a checkout from between 13 October and 11 November also prints `SynVoy v1.0.0`; the commit in the banner tells it apart from the release. These changes of that period matter when you compare with such a run:

- **Preset modules are the default** (10 November). `--modules auto` applies every preset module whose trigger fires. The chooser that picks one of the four presets is used only with `--modules legacy`; a preset you name yourself, with `--preset_override` or a `preset_*` profile, applies in any mode.
- **The tool versions are pinned** (10 November). `environment.yml` pins Nextflow, Python, parasail and the bioinformatics tools (MMseqs2, BLAST, miniprot, Augustus, Prodigal, MAFFT, IQ-TREE, samtools, NCBI datasets) to the tested versions; Java keeps a range, and Entrez Direct, ete3 and numpy have no version. Before, these tools had only lower bounds or none, so two machines could run different tool versions.
- **No silent failures** (30 October to 4 November). A Smith-Waterman, miniprot, Augustus or Prodigal run that does not finish within its time guard stops the task, instead of giving fewer hits or no model. A failed rescue, paralog check or ownership check is named in the report (`warnings`, `failed_steps`) instead of being skipped. The report also records the tool versions and resources of the run, and what each limit of the search dropped (`limits`).
- **One rule for every label** (15 October). HIGH, MEDIUM, AMBIGUOUS and LOW come from one judge: the E-value of the home protein in the place where the gene is expected, whether another home gene explains the model as well, and how much of the query the model covers. AMBIGUOUS means "needs curation" and is never counted. The labels of earlier runs rest on other rules.
- **One folder per home locus** (14 October) for the region files, `regions/<locus>/`. Before, the home loci of a run overwrote each other's region files.

## Before 13 October 2026

Until 13 October 2026 the version file said 2.0.0, so the launcher of that time printed `SynVoy v2.0.0`: such a run is older than 1.0.0, not newer. The checkouts of that time changed often. Two changes of that time matter most when you compare with such a run:

- **Distances in gene units** (11 October). The distances of the search follow the spacing of the flanking genes in each genome, instead of fixed numbers of base pairs. `-profile <execution>,legacy_distances` restores the fixed distances.
- **Figures** (5 to 11 October). Flanking genes are drawn with their real exons (5 October) and the anchor grid has a print version (7 October). From 11 October every searched genome gets a row, also one without a call.
