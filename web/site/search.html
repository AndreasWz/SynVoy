      How do you get the result?

      You get a private link right away. The page behind it shows the progress and then the result. Keep the link: it is the only way back to this search.

      Used for this one message and then deleted.

      What happens after you click

      1. The gene is located in its home genome.
      2. Its neighbouring genes are read from the annotation.
      3. Each chosen genome is searched for those neighbours.
      4. Your gene is searched for inside each neighbourhood found.
      5. Calls that match a related gene better are set aside.
      6. Figures and a summary are written.
